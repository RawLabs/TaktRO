# TaktRO Brand Summary

## Locked Direction
**Name:** TaktRO  
**Tagline:** **Less friction. More flow.**

## Primary Mark
- Automotive tachometer/gauge above the wordmark
- `Takt` in brushed silver/chrome
- `RO` in metallic gold
- Gold tach rim and needle
- Small redline accent
- Dark charcoal / black presentation field

## Brand Meaning
`Takt` refers to working pace/rhythm: useful work moving at a controlled rate.
`RO` anchors the first product in automotive repair-order and shop-management work.

The brand is intended to communicate:
- speed without chaos,
- controlled shop pace,
- reduced operator motion,
- efficient workflow,
- automotive heritage,
- professional operational software.

## Tagline
**Less friction. More flow.**

The tagline should remain exactly punctuated in normal brand use.

## Palette
- TaktRO Black: `#0A0A0B`
- Charcoal: `#111214`
- Gold: `#D4A017`
- Silver: `#C0C0C0`
- Redline Accent: `#E00014`
- White: `#F5F5F5`

## Usage Notes
- Prefer the dark logo treatment for screens, web headers, splash screens and social media.
- Keep the redline accent small; it is an accent, not a second primary brand color.
- Do not recolor `RO` away from gold in the primary mark.
- Do not stretch or distort the logo.
- Keep adequate clear space around the tach and wordmark.
- The tagline may be omitted where the logo is too small to remain legible.
- Use the tach-only mark for favicons/app icons.
